Privacy Policy

MadeInMinutes Uploader · Effective 21 November 2026

MadeInMinutes Uploader ("the tool") is a private, single-user script operated by the owner of the MadeInMinutes YouTube channel. It runs only on the owner's own computer and is not offered to the public. This policy explains how it handles data.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information accessed

With the account owner's authorization through Google OAuth, the tool accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel:

  • Basic channel information and statistics (channel title, subscriber, view and video counts).
  • The list of the channel's own uploaded videos and their public statistics.
  • Permission to upload new videos (and their titles, descriptions, tags and schedule) to that channel.

The tool does not access, collect or process data belonging to any other YouTube user.

How information is used

Information is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and to let the owner review their channel's performance. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage

OAuth authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer. Channel statistics are read on demand and are not stored on any server. No data is kept for longer than needed for these purposes, and no data is stored by the tool for more than 30 days without being refreshed from the API.

Sharing

No information obtained through YouTube API Services is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.

Cookies and tracking

The tool does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technology, and does not place information on users' devices.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Locally stored tokens can also be deleted from the computer running the tool.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
